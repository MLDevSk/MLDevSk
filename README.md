# 🧠 What I'm Building

<div align="center">

## ARTfulWAY

### Building AI-native systems for business execution.

</div>

ArtfulWay is evolving toward a broader AI execution platform, with **Nemi** as the core product direction.

### 🧩 Nemi — AI Product Manager

Nemi is being designed as an **AI Product Manager focused on business execution and orchestration**.

Instead of simply answering questions, the goal is to create a system that can:

```text
Business Goal
     ↓
Understand Context
     ↓
Break Down Objectives
     ↓
Create Execution Plan
     ↓
Coordinate Humans + AI Agents
     ↓
Track Progress
     ↓
Identify Blockers
     ↓
Adapt & Execute
```

### The long-term idea

> **Move AI from "answering" → "planning" → "coordinating" → "executing".**
